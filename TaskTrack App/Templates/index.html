TaskTrack Help Guide

Here’s a quick rundown of the parts that can be easy to miss when you first use the app.

How tasks are auto-sorted

Active tasks automatically sort by due date and time first, then by priority. The nearest deadlines appear earlier unless you manually drag tasks into your own custom order.

How drag-and-drop works

Drag any active task card if you want your own order. Once you do that, TaskTrack remembers your custom arrangement until you press Reset to Auto Order.

How upcoming and overdue work

Upcoming tasks are still pending and not yet late. Overdue tasks are already past their due date and time. In the Home section, yellow upcoming items are inside your urgency warning window.

How finished tasks work

When you mark a task as finished, it moves to the Finished Tasks view inside the Tasks page. You can undo one task there or use Mark All as Active if you want to bring everything back.

What undo buttons do

Important actions like add, delete, clear finished tasks, and reset order can show an Undo option for a few seconds. That lets you quickly recover from a mistake without re-entering everything.

What the urgency setting means

The urgency setting controls how many hours before a deadline a task starts being treated as urgent. That affects the yellow warning behavior in the Upcoming section.

Search and filtering

The search bar already acts like a filter. It looks through subject, task details, and priority. While search is active, drag-and-drop is paused so your saved order does not get mixed up by partial results.

Editing by clicking a card

You can click an active task card to edit it directly, or use the Edit Task button inside the card if you prefer a more obvious action.

Notes on each task card

Use the Add Notes or View Notes button inside any task card to save reminders, instructions, quiz writing topics, or materials. Reopen it anytime to keep writing or review what you already saved.

Progress ring

Each task can store a progress percentage from 0 to 100. The green donut ring on the card fills up based on how much of the task you have already done.

Checklist tab and finish animation

Inside the Notes popup, you can switch to the optional Checklist tab to track steps for that task. When you mark a task as finished, TaskTrack now plays a quick celebratory burst animation.
